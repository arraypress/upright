# Upright: privacy

Upright works entirely on your computer. It collects nothing and sends nothing.

## What it touches

The image or video you right-click or point at, and only when you choose a Rotate item or press one of its shortcuts. It turns it with the page’s own styling; it doesn’t read or save the picture. If you switch on single-key mode, it listens for R, L and 0 on the sites you chose, only while you point at an image or video and aren’t typing.

## What it keeps

Your settings, in Chrome’s own storage. If you’re signed in to Chrome with sync on, Chrome Sync keeps most of them in your Google account so your other computers get the same settings, as it does for Chrome’s own settings. Single-key mode and its sites stay on each computer. So does whether Chrome is in light or dark mode, used to draw the toolbar icon.

## Network

Upright makes no requests of its own. The viewer shows the image or video from its usual address, as the page did. To offer “Save rotated copy”, it asks the site for the image once more.

## No account, no analytics, no ads

Settings sync is Chrome’s own, through your Google account. Nobody, including ArrayPress, receives any of your data.
